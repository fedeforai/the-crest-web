Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ArmoFlow Ltd processes personal data in connection with its website, FrostDesk services, and The Crest Guild application/assessment service.

Last updated: 8 August 2026

1. Controller and scope

ArmoFlow Ltd acts as a data controller for personal data processed through www.armoflow.com, www.thecrestguild.com, and related corporate communications. For FrostDesk customer accounts, ArmoFlow Ltd may act as a data processor where clients determine the purposes and means of processing. For The Crest Guild application and assessment flows (candidatura form and positioning quiz), ArmoFlow Ltd acts as data controller.

2. Categories of data processed

Depending on use, we may process contact data, account and authentication data, service configuration data, support correspondence, messaging workflow metadata, calendar integration data, and operational data generated through day-to-day platform use.

For The Crest Guild specifically, we may also process:

  • Candidatura form: name, email address, current situation (free text), and optional message.
  • Positioning quiz: email address used to deliver the starting-rank result, plus quiz answers and derived rank needed to produce that result.
  • Site analytics (only with consent): aggregated usage events via Google Analytics 4 (page views, CTA clicks, quiz start/complete, form submit, outbound product links).

3. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

We process personal data under GDPR principles of lawfulness, fairness, and transparency. Legal bases include contract performance (service delivery), legitimate interests (platform security, abuse prevention, reliability, responding to candidature inquiries), consent (where required for optional analytics/cookies), and legal obligations (regulatory and record-keeping duties).

4. Crest Guild candidatura and quiz

The Crest Guild candidatura form collects the information needed to review interest in joining the Guild and to reply to applicants. Submissions are delivered through Formspree, which acts as a third-party processor for form intake.

The positioning quiz asks assessment questions in the browser and requests an email before showing the final starting rank, so the result can be delivered to you. Quiz lead emails are also submitted through Formspree. Quiz scoring runs in the browser; we do not use quiz answers for advertising.

FrostDesk messaging workflows and calendar integrations remain described in the ArmoFlow/FrostDesk product context and are not required to use The Crest Guild public site.

5. Third-party infrastructure providers

ArmoFlow Ltd uses vetted third-party infrastructure providers for hosting, storage, delivery, monitoring, and analytics. Such providers process data under contractual safeguards and data processing terms appropriate to their role.

For The Crest Guild website and application/assessment flows, third-party processors include in particular:

  • Formspree — candidatura form and quiz email delivery.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — measurement of site usage, loaded only after cookie/analytics consent where required.
  • Vercel — hosting and delivery of thecrestguild.com.

6. International transfers, retention, and security

Where data is transferred internationally, we apply suitable safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required. Data is retained only as long as necessary for service operation, legal obligations, and legitimate business needs. We maintain technical and organizational measures designed to protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

7. Data subject rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection to processing. Consent may be withdrawn where processing is consent-based. Requests can be sent to hello@armoflow.com.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes affecting ArmoFlow Ltd, FrostDesk, and The Crest Guild.

9. Contact

Privacy and data protection inquiries can be sent to hello@armoflow.com.

Related corporate policy (ArmoFlow / FrostDesk scope): armoflow.com/privacy.
