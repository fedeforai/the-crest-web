# Cookie Policy — The Crest Guild

**Effective date:** 8 August 2026
**Last updated:** 8 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how **ArmoFlow Ltd** ("**ArmoFlow**," "**we**") uses cookies and similar technologies on thecrestguild.com, in line with the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK/EU GDPR.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking scripts, which we refer to collectively as "cookies" in this Policy.

## 2. How we use cookies

### 2.1 Strictly necessary cookies
Required for the website to function and do not require consent under PECR.

| Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| The Crest Guild (consent) | Stores your cookie consent choice | Up to 12 months |
| Vercel | Infrastructure routing | Session |

### 2.2 Analytics cookies
Help us understand how visitors use thecrestguild.com. **Set only with your consent**, via Google Consent Mode v2 — analytics cookies are blocked until you actively accept them in our cookie banner.

| Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Google Analytics (GA4) | Website traffic and usage analytics | Up to 14 months (Google default) |

We do not currently use additional product-analytics or error-monitoring cookies on The Crest Guild (unlike FrostDesk, which uses PostHog and Sentry).

### 2.3 Application form and quiz
Our application form and quiz-result email capture are processed via **Formspree** (note: this differs from armoflow.com/FrostDesk, which uses Resend — The Crest Guild specifically uses Formspree). Formspree may set its own cookies when you submit a form. See Formspree's privacy policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 3. Managing your consent

When you first visit thecrestguild.com, a cookie banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time via the "Cookie Settings" / "Preferenze cookie" link in the website footer.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings; blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect site functionality (e.g. your consent choice not being remembered).
