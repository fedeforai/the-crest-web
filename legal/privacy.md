## 2. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal data when you:

- visit thecrestguild.com;
- take the positioning quiz;
- submit an application ("candidatura") to join the Guild;
- communicate with us by email or form.

Unlike FrostDesk, The Crest Guild does not connect to or process your own clients' messages, bookings, or communications — so this Policy does not need the customer/end-client distinction used in our FrostDesk Privacy Policy. Everyone using thecrestguild.com is, for the purposes of this Policy, a **visitor** or **applicant**.

## 3. What personal data we collect

### 3.1 Data you provide directly
- **Quiz**: your answers to the positioning quiz, and your email address, if you choose to provide it to receive your result.
- **Application ("candidatura")**: name, email address, your self-selected current situation (e.g. "In Scuola," "Base Camp," etc.), and any optional message you include.
- **Billing data** (where a fee applies at a given stage of the programme — see our Terms of Service): billing name and address, payment details. [DA CONFERMARE: quale processore di pagamento verrà utilizzato quando il pagamento sarà attivo, e a quale rango/tappa si applica il costo]
- **Communications**: messages you send us via email or contact form.

### 3.2 Data collected automatically
- Usage data and website analytics (pages visited, session duration, approximate location from IP address, device/browser type) via **Google Analytics (GA4)**. Analytics cookies are set only after you give consent through our cookie banner (Google Consent Mode v2).
- Cookies and similar technologies — see our separate **Cookie Policy**.

We do not currently use product analytics tools like PostHog or error-monitoring tools like Sentry on The Crest Guild — those are used on FrostDesk, not here.

### 3.3 Special category / sensitive data

We do not ask for or intentionally collect special category data (e.g. health data). The optional message field in the application form is free text — please do not include sensitive information there unless necessary; if you do, we will only use it to process your application.

## 7. Data retention

- **Application data (candidatura), if not accepted**: retained for 12 months to allow you to reapply and for our own process review, then deleted — mirrors ArmoFlow's account-data retention period.
- **Application data, if accepted**: retained for the duration of your participation in the programme, plus 12 months after you leave, for legal/accounting purposes.
- **Quiz email** (if provided only to receive your result, no application submitted): retained for 90 days then deleted, unless you separately apply to join — mirrors ArmoFlow's message-data retention period.
- **Billing records** (once applicable): retained for 7 years to meet UK tax and accounting obligations.
- **Analytics data**: retained for up to 26 months then deleted or aggregated.

- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal exceptions;
