The Guild's Ranks

From ski school to independence: five verifiable stages, not a list of courses. Each rank certifies something concrete you've built.

The full path

Explore every rank

Select a stage to see what it takes to get there and what actually changes in how you work.

Starting point

In School

Outside the Guild

You work only through the ski school: clients, rates, and schedule decided by someone else. This is where everyone who joins the Guild starts.

  • No clients of your own
  • Rate set by the school
  • Schedule decided by others
  • Zero risk, zero independence

Rank I

Base Camp

Completed

Admission to the Guild. First honest assessment: how much you depend on the school today, what assets you already have, where you can realistically start climbing.

  • Guild entry interview
  • Assessment of current assets
  • Personalised progression plan
  • Access to the instructor community

Rank II

Foundations

In progress

You put in place the infrastructure to say yes to a direct client. Without it, independence stays just an idea.

  • Active business registration
  • Professional insurance
  • Your own booking system
  • Your own price list

Rank III

Rope Team

Still to reach

You still work with the school, but start taking direct clients in parallel. Same rope, different lines.

  • First direct clients
  • Your own reputation (reviews, word of mouth)
  • Managing two channels
  • First real pricing comparisons

Rank IV

Summit

Final rank

Direct clients are enough to sustain the business. From here you can also come back to the Guild as a mentor.

  • Sufficient direct flow
  • Validation from a Guild mentor
  • Possibility of becoming a mentor
  • Name and reputation independent of the school

It is not a race for speed. It is a race for solidity: every rank you skip comes back later.

What moves you up a rank

Criteria, not time spent in the Guild

For the early ranks, objective criteria you can verify yourself are enough. For the last step, becoming a mentor, you also need peer validation.

01

Ranks I–III: objective checklist

Business registration, insurance, a booking system, first direct clients: things you can verify yourself, no outside judgement needed.

02

Rank IV: peer validation

For the Summit you need confirmation from a Guild mentor. At that level, the risk is declaring it yourself.

03

After the Summit: become a mentor

Whoever reaches real independence can come back to the Guild as a mentor for those climbing behind them.
